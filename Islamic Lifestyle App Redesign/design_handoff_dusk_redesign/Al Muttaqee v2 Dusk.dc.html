2a Direction “Dusk” · market-led redesign

আল মুত্তাকী Dusk

The reference set for this category is remarkably consistent: a deep dusk hero, gold on teal, generous radii, and a home screen that answers one question — how long until the next prayer. This direction takes that vocabulary and makes the app feel calm and premium instead of utilitarian, while keeping every feature and flow from the previous board.

38px device, 28px hero sheet, 22px cards, 999px chips. Nothing sharp — the app should feel held, not filed.
Ornament
One motif only: the eight-point khatim star, as a 10% texture inside the hero. Never as a border, never as an illustration.
Gold discipline
Gold is for the live value and the single primary action. On ivory it darkens to #8A6A0F so small Bangla text still reads.

Build order
Phase 1: five-tab nav, prayer screen, tracking, adhan reminders — touches only dashboard, prayer_times, notifications. Phase 2: Quran reader, reading plan, dua, Ramadan. Phase 3: jamaat crowd-sourcing, widgets.
Flutter notes
The hero gradient is one Container with a DecoratedBox + tiled Image.asset for the khatim texture at 12% opacity. Cards are Card with BorderRadius.circular(22) and a single soft shadow — replace AppColors.brand* with the Dusk ramp and the whole app shifts at once.
Accessibility held
Ivory on deep teal ≈ 12:1; gold on teal ≈ 7:1; gold darkens to #8A6A0F on ivory. Bangla body never below 12.5 px, tap targets ≥ 44 px — the 45+ audience was the constraint that set these floors.
