Redesign

Twenty screens, a restructured five-tab architecture, and a feature roadmap — rebuilt on a flat modular grid: no rounded corners, 2 px rules doing the organising, one red accent reserved for the single thing that matters on each screen.

What the app has, and what it's missing

Read from the Flutter source. The engine is in good shape — adhan-based prayer calculation with ten methods, Hanafi madhab toggle and per-prayer offsets; a Quran module with reciters, per-surah offline download, repeat and playback speed; bundled hadith and daily-content JSON. What's thin is everything around it: no habit loop, no reason to open the app twice a day, and every screen is a white card on cream with no hierarchy.

Where the red goes

Exactly one red field per screen, on the thing the user came for: the countdown on হোম, the payable amount on যাকাত, the iftar clock in রমজান. Everything else is ink on ground.

Bangla-first sizing

Bangla runs taller than Latin — body copy sits at 14 px with 1.7 line-height, never below 12.5 px, and no tab label is shorter than four characters so the 5-tab bar still reads on a 360 dp screen. Every tap target is ≥ 44 px.

What to build first

Phase 1 is nav + prayer screen + tracking + reminders — it changes retention on its own, and it touches only dashboard, prayer_times and notifications. Quran reading changes land in phase 2.
